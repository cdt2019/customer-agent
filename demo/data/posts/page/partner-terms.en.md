By applying to become an Example Store partner, you confirm that you have read and agree to this agreement, which supplements the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms). Commission rates, level thresholds, holding days, the minimum withdrawal and fees are as shown at the time on the [partner page](/en/partner) and in your partner centre.

The Chinese version of this agreement is the one that applies; this English version is provided for convenience.

## Joining

- Members apply on the [partner page](/en/partner), saying where and how they will promote. Once approved, you get a referral link and code; you can change the code once yourself.
- Joining is free and there is no recruiting chain: there is only one level, and you earn only on sales you bring.
- If an application is declined, you can apply again after 30 days.

## Which orders count for you

- After a friend clicks your link, orders placed in the same browser within 30 days count for you; if they click another partner's link in between, the later click counts.
- Once a friend signs up through your link, every later order of theirs counts for you.
- These orders earn no commission:
  - orders placed with your own account, or with an email you use or have used;
  - orders placed through the open API;
  - orders brought while your partnership is suspended.
- Orders from a network you use (an IP you signed in from recently) are counted only after a manual check, and not if the check fails.

## How commission is calculated

- Each item counts separately: what was paid for it (after discounts) minus its actual cost, times your level's rate. The shop may instead calculate on the amount paid; the partner page says which. Some products may have their own rate, a fixed amount per item, or no commission; the partner centre shows this.
- Costs are calculated by us from actual purchase prices and are not published; the partner centre shows how much you earn per item for each product. When calculating on profit, products with unknown cost earn no commission.
- Commission is calculated at delivery using the rules and rate at that time; later rule changes do not affect commission already earned.
- When your settled referral sales reach a threshold you move up a level automatically, and orders delivered afterwards use the new rate; refunds never move you down. We may also adjust your level manually.

## Holding, crediting and refunds

- Commission is held after the order is delivered, for the longer of the holding days and the product's warranty days, then credited to "withdrawable commission" automatically.
- When a friend gets a refund, commission is reversed in proportion to the refund against what was paid for that item; a full refund of the order reverses it all. Commission not yet credited is reduced directly; credited commission is taken from your withdrawable commission, and any shortfall becomes a debt that later commission pays off first.
- Withdrawals are paused while you have a debt.

## Withdrawals

- Once withdrawable commission reaches the minimum, you can withdraw once per day (Beijing time; declined requests do not count), with the fee per withdrawal shown on the partner page.
- Payout methods: WeChat Pay (receiving code and real name), Alipay (account and real name), USDT (BEP20 network). Setting or changing a payout account requires your sign-in password; it can be used for withdrawals after 24 hours, and we email you about the change.
- We review and pay out by hand, recording the transaction hash or reference in the withdrawal history. USDT is converted at the rate at payout time; the amount actually sent is the one recorded.
- Losses from wrong payout details are yours, so please check them carefully.
- Withdrawable commission can also be moved into your shopping balance at any time, at least ¥1 each time. Once moved it follows the balance rules and cannot be moved back or withdrawn.

## Promotion rules

- Describe products truthfully, do not exaggerate, do not promise anything the product page does not, and do not pose as this site or the official platform.
- No spam, no harassment, and no promotion in illegal settings or against platform rules.
- No buying from yourself, fake orders, multiple accounts or other people's identities to earn commission, and no cash-back schemes that lead people to order and then ask for refunds.
- Never ask anyone for account passwords, verification codes or sign-in data, and never offer signing in for others, topping up on their behalf, phone-number verification or API relays in this site's name.
- If these rules are broken, we may reject the commission involved, suspend or end your partnership, and recover commission already paid.

## Other

- You declare and pay any taxes due on income from promotion yourself.
- Private arrangements between you and your friends (such as cash back) have nothing to do with this site.
- When we change the rules we update the partner page and this agreement; changes apply only to orders delivered afterwards.
