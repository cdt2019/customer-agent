## Ordering and paying

- Pay within the time shown once an order is created (usually 15 minutes). Unpaid orders close automatically and the stock is released.
- Some payment methods carry a channel fee charged by the payment provider. It is listed next to the payment method and in the order summary at checkout, before you pay. Paying from the balance has no fee.
- Pay the amount shown, using the latest payment code on the page. After switching payment method, do not pay an older code. For USDT, send the amount and use the network shown at the cashier.
- Money received after an order timed out or was cancelled: if the stock and offer allowance are still there, the order reopens and is delivered automatically; if not, customer service handles it: delivering once restocked, delivering after you pay the difference, or refunding.
- An underpaid order is not delivered. For a member's order, if this payment method can also be used for top-ups, the money received goes into your balance in proportion to the item amount (the channel fee you paid is not included) and the order still waits for payment; otherwise customer service checks and contacts you.
- Overpayment: the order goes ahead as usual. On a member's order, if this payment method can also be used for top-ups, the extra goes into your balance automatically; otherwise an extra under ¥1 is not refunded, and more than that is checked and refunded by customer service.
- Paying twice, or paying an old payment code: customer service checks and refunds the extra, or, for members, moves it into the balance; there is nothing to request.

## Codes activated on the redeem page

Products whose "Delivery" reads "A code, activated on the redeem page" (for example ChatGPT memberships) work like this:

- On the order page, choose "Redeem" next to the code. On the redeem page, paste your own account's sign-in data (session), check the account, then confirm.
- The sign-in data is passed to the activation service only to check the account, activate it and look up the subscription. This site does not store it or write it to logs. We keep the email of the account activated and a fingerprint of the sign-in data (a digest that cannot be turned back into it), for after-sales checks. Your account password is never needed.
- Auto-renewal is turned off as part of activation, so nothing is charged when the period ends.
- Codes do not expire. Unredeemed codes can be refunded. Once redeemed, a code is not refundable for a change of mind; the warranty runs from the moment it is redeemed. See the [After-sales and Refund Policy](/en/legal/refund).
- Check the account email shown on the redeem page before you activate: once activation succeeds, the code is used.
- The subscription is paid by the activation service with a payment method from outside your region. This may not comply with the subscription platform's (for example OpenAI's) terms, and the platform may cancel the subscription or restrict the account. If that happens within the warranty, it is handled under the warranty.
