The Chinese version of this policy is the one that applies; this English version is provided for convenience.

## How to ask

Open the order page, choose "Ask for support", pick the kind of problem (Warranty replacement, Refund request, Other question), describe it, and attach up to 3 screenshots per message. When customer service replies we email you, and you can continue on the ticket page. Customer service hours are on the [Contact support](/en/help/contact) page.

- One order can have at most 3 open tickets at a time.
- Once solved, you can close the ticket yourself; a ticket marked "Resolved" by staff closes automatically after 7 days without a new reply. A new problem after that can be raised again from the order page.

## Warranty

- Each product's warranty days are shown on the product and order pages and run from the **moment of delivery**; for codes activated on the redeem page, from the **moment of successful redemption**. A request made within the warranty is handled as covered even if the warranty has ended by the time staff handle it.
- Within the warranty, for problems the product page describes (such as an invalid code, an account that cannot sign in, a subscription that stopped), we **replace** first, replacing only the affected item. A replacement does not restart the warranty.
- Each item can be replaced a limited number of times within its warranty, set per product, usually 2. Beyond that, or when no replacement is possible, we refund in proportion or in full.
- Items already refunded are not replaced.
- Not covered by the warranty:
  - changing the account's password, email or linked details;
  - sharing or reselling the account;
  - bans or restrictions caused by frequent IP changes or breaking the platform's rules;
  - problems from not following the product page's instructions;
  - anything after the warranty ends.

## Codes activated on the redeem page

- **Unredeemed**: can be refunded. With several in one order, you can tick which ones when asking; if none are ticked, customer service decides. Refunded codes are cancelled.
- **Being activated, or result being checked**: cannot be refunded until the result is known.
- **Redeemed**: not refundable for a change of mind. If the subscription stops within the warranty, customer service checks and issues a new code for you to activate again, or refunds by the days left:
  - at most the amount paid for that code × days left ÷ warranty days, rounded down to the fen. For example, with a 30-day warranty, ¥99 paid and 10 days used, up to ¥66.00;
  - days used run from the moment of redemption to when you open the ticket, with any part of a day counting as a full day;
  - after the refund, the code is cancelled and no longer replaced.

## Refunds

- **Items not yet delivered** (for example a manually delivered item still in progress): can be refunded in full; if every item in the order is refunded, the order is cancelled.
- **Delivered items**: codes and accounts count as used once delivered and in principle are not refundable for a change of mind; where the warranty applies but no replacement is possible, we refund under the product page's rules (for example by days left). For codes activated on the redeem page, see the section above.
- In an order with several items you can refund just some of them, up to what you paid for those items; when every item is refunded, up to everything you paid for the order (fees included).
- **Fees**: when a refund is our fault (for example out of stock or wrong delivery), the payment channel fee is refunded in proportion; for refunds for other reasons it is not.
- Refund methods:
  - **To the original payment method**: when the payment channel supports it, back to the Alipay, WeChat Pay or other account you paid with; timing depends on the payment platform;
  - **To your balance**: available for members' orders, arriving immediately. Orders paid from the balance are refunded to the balance;
  - **Transfer by customer service**: in other cases (including USDT payments), customer service sends it by Alipay, WeChat Pay, USDT or similar to an account confirmed with you in the ticket; timing depends on that platform.
- We email you when a refund is complete; a fully refunded order no longer shows its codes.
- Coupon uses and limited-time allowances taken by the order are not returned after a refund.

## Special cases

- **Paying after timeout**: money received after the order closed is delivered automatically if the stock and offer allowance are still there; otherwise customer service handles it: delivering once restocked, delivering after you pay the difference, or refunding.
- **Overpaying**: on a member's order the extra goes into the balance automatically (when the payment method can also be used for top-ups); otherwise an extra under ¥1 is not refunded, and more than that is checked and refunded by customer service.
- **Paying twice, underpaying**: customer service checks and refunds the extra (members can also have it moved into the balance) or asks you to pay the difference; there is nothing to request, and questions can go through "Ask for support" on the order page. A member's underpayment may already have been moved into the balance under the [Terms of Service](/en/legal/terms).

If you disagree with how something was handled, you can keep discussing it in the ticket.
