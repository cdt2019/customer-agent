We collect only what is needed for transactions, after-sales and member features, and as little of it as we can.

The Chinese version of this policy is the one that applies; this English version is provided for convenience.

## What we collect

| Information | When | What for |
|---|---|---|
| Email | When ordering or signing up | Sending order links, verification codes, delivery and refund notices; finding orders |
| Order details (products, amounts, payment method and time) | When ordering and paying | Delivery, after-sales, accounting |
| Answers to an order form | When a product asks for them (for example an email to receive an invitation) | Delivering that product |
| IP address, browser details and rough location | When ordering, signing up, signing in, opening a ticket | Preventing abuse and fraud, keeping accounts safe, handling disputes |
| Ticket messages and screenshots | When you ask for help | Solving your problem |
| Member nickname, password and sign-in history | When signing up and in | Signing in and account security. Passwords are stored only as a digest that cannot be reversed; we cannot see them either |
| Two-step verification secret | When you turn it on | Checking the numbers from your authenticator |
| Emails you have used | When a member changes email | Stopping partners from earning commission on their own orders |
| Balance and commission records | On top-ups, payments, refunds, commission and withdrawals | Bookkeeping |
| A partner's application and payout accounts (WeChat Pay receiving code, Alipay account or USDT address, with real name) | When applying and setting payout accounts | Reviewing applications, paying out |
| Referral source | When you arrive through a partner's link (the link, time, IP and landing page) | Crediting the order to the partner who referred you; referral statistics |
| Visit statistics (a random visitor ID, pages and products viewed, device type, referring site's domain) | While you browse | Seeing which products and articles are read, improving the site. No IP is recorded and nothing goes to third parties |
| Open API purpose, IP allow-list and call logs | When applying for and using the open API | Review, authentication, troubleshooting |
| Account email, sign-in data fingerprint and IP at redemption | When you check an account or activate on the redeem page | Activation and after-sales checks |

Screenshots are re-encoded on upload (removing location and similar data) and stored encrypted. Codes, two-step verification secrets and payout accounts are also encrypted; staff see a full payout account when paying out, and every view is logged.

We **do not collect** your account passwords or verification codes. Please do not put them in tickets.

## Sign-in data at redemption

When activating a membership such as ChatGPT on the redeem page, you paste your own account's sign-in data (session):

- It goes from your browser through this site to the activation service only in three kinds of request: checking the account, activating, and looking up the subscription. It is discarded after use: never stored in the database, logs, audit records or alerts.
- What we keep: the email of the account activated (staff see it in full; the progress page shows it masked), a fingerprint of the sign-in data (a digest that cannot be reversed, used to tell whether two requests used the same data), the IP the request came from, and the activation service's reply.
- The redeem page sets no cookies. Your browser keeps only the last 3 redemption numbers and your language choice, never codes or sign-in data.
- Once activation is done, you can sign out of all devices in ChatGPT's settings, and the sign-in data stops working.

## Cookies and browser storage

| Name | Purpose | Kept for |
|---|---|---|
| Order access | Remembers which orders this device can open (the latest 30). Without it, orders open only from the email link | 90 days, extended with each new order; removed when you sign out or clear it under "Find an order" |
| Sign-in | Keeps members signed in | 30 days, extended while you keep visiting; removed when you sign out |
| Visitor ID | A random ID for counting visits; when you arrive through a partner's link, also used to credit the order to that partner | 90 days, extended on each visit; orders count only clicks from the last 30 days |
| Language | Remembers Chinese or English | 1 year |
| Theme | Remembers light or dark | 1 year; removed when set back to "Follow system" |
| Cart (browser storage) | Remembers what is in your cart | Until you empty the cart |

We use no advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

## Who we share with

Only as far as a transaction needs it, with these service providers:

- **Payment providers**: process payments and refunds to the original method, receiving the order number and amount; some channels (such as Epay) also receive the paying device's IP for their own risk control;
- **The activation service**: receives your sign-in data when you activate on the redeem page, to check the account, activate and look up the subscription;
- **Suppliers**: some goods come from upstream suppliers; we order from them with only the product and quantity, none of your information;
- **Email providers**: send verification codes and notices for us, receiving your email address and the message (emails contain no codes);
- **Cloudflare**: speeds up and protects the site and runs the human check; all requests to this site pass through it;
- **Telegram**: notifies shop staff of things to handle, with the order number, amount, product name, ticket type and title and similar, never codes; emails are masked.

We do not sell your information. Where laws and regulations require it, we provide it as required.

## Emails we send

- Emails tied directly to your orders and account: order links, delivery, refunds, ticket replies, verification codes, security notices.
- Only two other kinds: a notice that the shop has given you a coupon, and a reminder that a monthly membership you bought is about to end (off by default). Both carry an unsubscribe link; unsubscribing stops them without affecting the emails above.

## How long we keep it

- Orders, after-sales, balance, commission, withdrawal and email records: for after-sales, accounting and legal retention, at least 3 years after the transaction.
- Ticket screenshots: deleted 180 days after the ticket closes.
- Sign-in history and site messages: 180 days.
- Referral clicks: 400 days, with IP and landing page cleared after 90 days.
- Detailed visit statistics: 30 days, after which only daily totals with no personal information remain.
- Open API call logs: 30 days.
- Verification codes: deleted 1 day after expiry.

## Your rights

- Members can change nickname, email and password and turn two-step verification on or off in the account centre at any time.
- Through [Contact support](/en/help/contact) you can ask to see, correct or delete information tied to your email (except transaction records the law requires us to keep).
